HOUSING

current problems

WASTED RESOURCES

Data centres produce huge amounts of heat that is often released into the air instead of being reused. At the same time, neighbourhoods consume energy that could be supplied by these wasted resources.

LACK OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING

Rising rents and property prices make it difficult for many people to find a decent home. In many cities, housing has become an investment rather than a basic human need.

POOR LIVING CONDITIONS

Poor insulation, neglected buildings and limited access to essential services can harm people's health and well-being. Everyone deserves a safe, comfortable and dignified place to live.

possible solutions

TURN WASTE INTO ENERGY

Infomaniak's AI data centre in Switzerland recovers its waste heat to warm nearby homes. Similar projects already exist in Finland, proving that energy can be reused instead of wasted.

QUALITY HOMES FOR ALL

Completed in 2023 in Son Servera, Spain, this social housing project shows that affordable homes can also be beautiful, welcoming and built to a high environmental standard.

ECO-DISTRICTS

Eco-districts combine sustainable design with better quality of life by creating neighbourhoods that are greener, more connected and more resilient. They aim to address the three pillars of sustainable development :

  • Social accessible public spaces and community services
  • Economic local shops, jobs and mixed-use development
  • Environmental energy-efficient buildings and renewable energy
