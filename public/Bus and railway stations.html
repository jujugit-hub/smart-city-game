BUS AND RAILWAY STATIONS

current problems

TOO MANY CARS

Private cars spend around 95% of their time parked, yet cities devote huge amounts of public space to roads and parking. They also create traffic jams, noise and air pollution that affect everyone's quality of life.

UNRELIABLE TRANSPORT

Delays, cancellations and limited routes discourage many people from using public transport. When buses or trains are unavailable, many people feel they have no alternative but to drive.

THE COST OF MOBILITY

Transport should not prevent people from studying, working or meeting others. Expensive tickets and subscriptions can make sustainable travel inaccessible for many households.

possible solutions

WALKABLE CITIES

A sustainable city is designed so that most daily needs are within a 15-minute walk or bike ride. With fewer cars, streets become safer, quieter and can be transformed into parks, housing or public spaces.

SMART PUBLIC TRANSPORT

One tram can carry more than 200 passengers while using far less street space than the same number of cars. Combined with clean buses and shared bikes, frequent public transport offers a reliable alternative for everyone.

FREE PUBLIC TRANSPORT

Several European cities already provide free public transport for residents. Germany's Deutschlandticket, available for 63€ per month, also shows that affordable public transport can be implemented on a large scale.
