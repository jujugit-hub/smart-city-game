TOWN HALL

current problems

POLITICS FOR THE FEW

Running for local office often requires time, money and flexibility. Many people simply can't afford it, making elected representatives less diverse than the communities they serve.

UNEQUAL PAY

A mayor of a small municipality can receive less than €1,100 a month, making public service difficult for people without independent financial security.

LACK OF REPRESENTATION

Women hold only around 20% of mayoral positions, while people under 30 remain extremely rare in local government. Many voices are still missing from public decision-making.

possible solutions

POLITICS FOR EVERYONE

Better compensation and stronger public support would allow more citizens to run for office. A council should reflect its population, not only those with free time and money.

FAIR PAY

Local representatives should earn enough to serve their communities without financial hardship. Public service should be accessible, whatever the size of the municipality.

MORE DIVERSE LEADERSHIP

Fighting sexism and encouraging civic education can broaden participation in politics. Democracy is stronger when everyone is represented.
