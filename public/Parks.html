PARKS

current problems

NOT ENOUGH GREEN SPACE

In Paris, accessible public green spaces cover less than 9 m² per resident. Many people grow up with limited access to nature, despite its proven benefits for health and well-being.

DIRTY AIR

Road traffic, heating systems and industry release pollutants that harm our health. Air pollution is one of Europe's leading environmental health risks, especially in large cities.

URBAN HEAT

Concrete and asphalt absorb heat throughout the day, making cities much hotter during summer. This urban heat island effect becomes more dangerous as heatwaves become more frequent.

possible solutions

GREEN SPACES FOR HEALTH

Parks encourage exercise, reduce stress and improve mental health. More trees and gardens mean healthier communities and better places for people to meet and relax.

NATURAL CARBON SINKS

Trees absorb and store carbon while filtering many air pollutants. A single tree can provide benefits for decades, making cities cleaner and more resilient.

NATURAL COOLING

Planting trees, green roofs and climbing plants creates shade and cools the surrounding air. Nature is one of the most effective cooling systems for adapting cities to climate change.
