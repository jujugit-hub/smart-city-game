SCHOOLS

current problems

ONE SIZE FITS ALL

Many schools still rely on long lectures and standardised teaching. Every student learns differently, yet creativity, cooperation and practical skills often receive too little attention.

UNDERVALUED TEACHERS

Teachers shape the next generation but often work with limited resources and heavy workloads. When teachers struggle, students do too, making quality education harder to provide.

UNEQUAL OPPORTUNITIES

A student's social background still has a major influence on academic success. Where you are born should not decide your future, yet inequalities persist throughout the education system.

possible solutions

STUDENT-CENTRED LEARNING

Nordic education systems give schools greater autonomy and encourage teamwork and personalised learning. Finland consistently ranks among the world's top education systems.

  • More autonomy for schools and teachers
  • More collaborative projects and active learning
  • Individual support adapted to each student

SUPPORT TEACHERS

Better salaries, more classroom resources and smaller workloads would improve learning conditions for everyone. Education is an investment, not a cost.

EQUAL CHANCES FOR ALL

Every child should have access to the same opportunities, regardless of their family's income. Support should go where needs are greatest.

  • Early support for struggling students
  • More resources for disadvantaged schools
  • Free meals, supplies and inclusive public education
