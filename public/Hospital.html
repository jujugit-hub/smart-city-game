HOSPITAL

current problems

STAFF SHORTAGES

Poor working conditions and low pay make it difficult to recruit and keep healthcare workers. As a result, many hospitals are forced to close beds because there are simply not enough staff to care for patients.

HARDER TO GET CARE

40% of French people feel that access to healthcare has worsened. Long waiting times and medical deserts make it harder for many patients to receive treatment when they need it.

HIGH CARBON FOOTPRINT

Medicines and medical devices account for around half of a hospital's carbon footprint. Healthcare saves lives, but it must also reduce waste and pollution whenever possible.

possible solutions

BETTER WORKING CONDITIONS

Better salaries, safer staffing levels and increased public funding would make hospital careers more attractive. Investing in healthcare workers means investing in better care for everyone.

CARE CLOSER TO HOME

Telemedicine and local health centres can reduce pressure on hospitals by treating simple cases remotely. This allows emergency services and specialists to focus on the patients who need them most.

GREENER HEALTHCARE

Reusing equipment when safe, reducing unnecessary tests and choosing sustainable products can lower emissions without reducing the quality of care. Better healthcare can also be better for the planet.
